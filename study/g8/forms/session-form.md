# G-8 session form

Filled in by the participant during the session. The moderator copies this file into the session
folder (`npm run study -- g8-new-session`) and fills in the header and the requested behaviours
before the session starts. Write no name, e-mail address or other personal detail in this form:
you appear only by your participant code.

- Participant code: <P.. — filled in by the moderator>
- Session: <1 or 2 — filled in by the moderator>
- Change: <A, B, C or D — filled in by the moderator>
- Method: <review assistant / standard diff review — filled in by the moderator>

## How to fill in this form

1. Review the change in your own way and at your own pace. The session ends at your last decision,
   or at 3 hours.
2. In section 1, decide for the change as a whole and for each requested behaviour whether you
   accept it: write `accept` or `reject` in the second column. A reason is optional.
3. In section 2, write down every defect you find, one per row, as soon as you find it. Add rows
   as needed (`F4`, `F5`, …). File and lines help the raters but are optional; the description is
   what counts.
4. Tell the moderator when you take a break and when you have made your last decision.
