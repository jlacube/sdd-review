We are comparing two ways of reviewing large code changes written by coding agents: a review
assistant and a standard diff review in your usual diff viewer. We measure how long a review takes
and how many defects reviewers find. **We are measuring the methods, not you.**

## What you will do

- Two sessions on two different days, at most 7 days apart. In each session you review one code
  change, of 2,000 to 10,000 changed lines.
- In one session you use the review assistant. A 30-minute tutorial on a practice change comes
  directly before that session. In the other session you use your usual diff viewer, with no AI
  assistance.
- You decide whether to accept the change and each requested behaviour, and you write down every
  defect you find on a session form.
- You work at your own pace. A session ends at your last decision, or at 3 hours. You can take
  breaks, and they are not counted.
