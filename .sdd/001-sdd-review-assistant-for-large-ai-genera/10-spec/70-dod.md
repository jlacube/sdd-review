# C001 — Definition of Done

CHG-001 is done when every item below and every inherited global item of §1 holds, each with its
evidence attached to the change. Requirements: `20-capabilities/*/requirements.md`; NFRs and their
reference environment: `30-nfr/nfr.md`; comparison protocol: `30-nfr/nfr.md` §3.

## 1. Global DoD
Inherited global items of `.sdd/_constitution/constitution.md` §Global DoD, all of which apply:

- **DOD-G-01** — applies to the whole automated suite of CHG-001, including the acceptance tests
  of C001-DOD-001 to C001-DOD-009 and the security and reliability tests of §2.
- **DOD-G-02** — applies to the 215 active requirements of C001-CAP-01 to C001-CAP-09, one per
  requirement heading of their `requirements.md` files (C001-REQ-813 and C001-REQ-903 are
  retired); the trace
  report is `80-traceability.md` regenerated by `sdd trace 001 --write` at the closing commit.
- **DOD-G-03** — applies to every code change of the tasks of `20-plan/50-tasks/`.
- **DOD-G-04** — applies to the languages and formatters the plan selects for the analysis core,
  the CLI and the review UI.
- **DOD-G-05** — applies to the living specs written by `sdd archive` for C001-CAP-01 to
  C001-CAP-09 and to the user documentation of C001-DOD-032.
